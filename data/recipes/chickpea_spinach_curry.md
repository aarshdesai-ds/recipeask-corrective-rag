## Ingredients
- spinach: 200 g
- chickpeas: 2 can
- coconut milk: 400 ml
- onion: 1 pcs
- garlic: 3 clove
- curry powder: 2 tbsp
- rice: 150 g

## Method
1. Fry the onion and garlic for 5 minutes, then stir in the curry powder.
2. Add the chickpeas and coconut milk and simmer for 10 minutes.
3. Stir in the spinach until it wilts. Serve with the rice.

## Notes
Uses a full bag of spinach.
