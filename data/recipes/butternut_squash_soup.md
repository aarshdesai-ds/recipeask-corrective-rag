## Ingredients
- butternut squash: 1 pcs
- onion: 1 pcs
- cream: 100 ml
- vegetable stock: 750 ml

## Method
1. Roast the cubed squash for 25 minutes.
2. Simmer with the onion and stock for 10 minutes.
3. Blend and stir in the cream.

## Notes
Leave out the cream for a lighter soup.
