## Ingredients
- eggs: 4 pcs
- chopped tomatoes: 1 can
- red pepper: 1 pcs
- onion: 1 pcs
- cumin: 1 tsp

## Method
1. Soften the onion and pepper with the cumin.
2. Add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
3. Make four wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook for 6 minutes.

## Notes
Good with bread.
