## Ingredients
- chicken thighs: 500 g
- green curry paste: 2 tbsp
- coconut milk: 400 ml
- green beans: 150 g
- fish sauce: 1 tbsp
- rice: 150 g

## Method
1. Fry the curry paste for a minute, then add the chicken.
2. Pour in the coconut milk and simmer for 15 minutes, adding the beans for the last 5.
3. Season with the fish sauce and serve with the rice.

## Notes
Fish sauce is made from fish.
