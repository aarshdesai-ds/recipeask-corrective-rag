## Ingredients
- tofu: 300 g
- broccoli: 200 g
- soy sauce: 3 tbsp
- garlic: 2 clove
- ginger: 1 tbsp
- rice: 150 g

## Method
1. Press the tofu, cut it into cubes and fry until golden.
2. Add the broccoli, garlic and ginger and stir-fry for 4 minutes.
3. Add the soy sauce and serve with the rice.

## Notes
Soy sauce contains wheat, so this dish contains gluten.
