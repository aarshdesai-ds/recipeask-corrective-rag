## Ingredients
- lasagne sheets: 200 g
- spinach: 100 g
- ricotta: 250 g
- passata: 500 ml
- mozzarella: 125 g

## Method
1. Layer the passata, sheets, spinach and ricotta.
2. Top with the mozzarella.
3. Bake for 35 minutes.

## Notes
Quicker than a classic lasagne, but it still needs 35 minutes in the oven.
