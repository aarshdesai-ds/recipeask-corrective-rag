## Ingredients
- cod fillets: 2 pcs
- chorizo: 100 g
- cherry tomatoes: 200 g
- potatoes: 500 g

## Method
1. Roast the potatoes and chorizo for 15 minutes.
2. Add the cod and tomatoes and roast for 12 more minutes.

## Notes
Check the chorizo label if cooking for coeliacs.
