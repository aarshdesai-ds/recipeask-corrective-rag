## Ingredients
- eggs: 4 pcs
- spinach: 30 g
- feta: 60 g
- butter: 10 g

## Method
1. Beat the eggs.
2. Melt the butter, add the eggs and scatter over the spinach and feta.
3. Fold and serve.

## Notes
Uses only a handful of spinach.
