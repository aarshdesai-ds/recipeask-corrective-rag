## Ingredients
- chicken breast: 400 g
- red pepper: 2 pcs
- onion: 1 pcs
- flour tortillas: 6 pcs
- fajita seasoning: 2 tbsp

## Method
1. Slice the chicken, peppers and onion.
2. Fry with the seasoning for 10 minutes.
3. Serve in the warm tortillas.

## Notes
Uses flour tortillas.
