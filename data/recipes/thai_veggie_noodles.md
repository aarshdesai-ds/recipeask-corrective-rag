## Ingredients
- rice noodles: 200 g
- peanuts: 50 g
- soy sauce: 2 tbsp
- carrot: 1 pcs
- red pepper: 1 pcs
- lime: 1 pcs

## Method
1. Soak the noodles for 5 minutes and drain.
2. Stir-fry the carrot and pepper for 3 minutes, then add the noodles and soy sauce.
3. Top with crushed peanuts and lime juice.

## Notes
The peanuts go on at the end.
