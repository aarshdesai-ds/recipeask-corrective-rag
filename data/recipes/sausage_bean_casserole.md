## Ingredients
- sausages: 6 pcs
- cannellini beans: 1 can
- chopped tomatoes: 1 can
- onion: 1 pcs

## Method
1. Brown the sausages.
2. Add the onion, tomatoes and beans and simmer for 30 minutes.

## Notes
Most supermarket sausages contain rusk, which is made from wheat.
