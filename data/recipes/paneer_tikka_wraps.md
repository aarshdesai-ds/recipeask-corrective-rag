## Ingredients
- paneer: 250 g
- yogurt: 100 g
- flour tortillas: 4 pcs
- red onion: 1 pcs
- tikka paste: 2 tbsp

## Method
1. Coat the paneer in the yogurt and tikka paste.
2. Grill for 8 minutes, turning once.
3. Wrap with sliced onion.

## Notes
Can be made the night before.
