## Ingredients
- puff pastry: 320 g
- mushrooms: 500 g
- spinach: 100 g
- walnuts: 50 g
- onion: 1 pcs
- thyme: 1 tsp

## Method
1. Cook the mushrooms, onion and thyme until all the liquid has gone, then stir in the chopped walnuts.
2. Lay the spinach on the pastry, add the filling and roll it up.
3. Bake for 45 minutes and rest for 10 before slicing.

## Notes
A dinner-party main. Check the pastry is dairy-free.
