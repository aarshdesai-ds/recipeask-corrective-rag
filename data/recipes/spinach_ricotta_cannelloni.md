## Ingredients
- spinach: 250 g
- ricotta: 250 g
- cannelloni tubes: 200 g
- passata: 500 ml
- parmesan: 30 g

## Method
1. Wilt the spinach, squeeze it dry and mix it with the ricotta.
2. Fill the tubes and lay them in the passata.
3. Top with parmesan and bake for 35 minutes.

## Notes
Uses a lot of spinach.
