## Ingredients
- arborio rice: 200 g
- mushrooms: 300 g
- parmesan: 50 g
- white wine: 100 ml
- vegetable stock: 1000 ml
- butter: 30 g

## Method
1. Fry the mushrooms in half the butter and set aside.
2. Toast the rice, add the wine, then add the stock a ladle at a time for 25 minutes.
3. Stir in the mushrooms, the parmesan and the rest of the butter.

## Notes
Parmesan is traditionally made with animal rennet.
