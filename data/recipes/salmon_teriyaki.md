## Ingredients
- salmon fillets: 2 pcs
- soy sauce: 3 tbsp
- honey: 1 tbsp
- ginger: 1 tbsp
- rice: 150 g
- broccoli: 200 g

## Method
1. Mix the soy sauce, honey and ginger.
2. Pan-fry the salmon for 6 minutes, add the sauce and cook for 2 more.
3. Serve with the rice and steamed broccoli.

## Notes
Soy sauce contains wheat.
