## Ingredients
- red lentils: 200 g
- carrot: 2 pcs
- onion: 1 pcs
- cumin: 1 tsp
- vegetable stock: 1000 ml
- lemon: 1 pcs

## Method
1. Soften the onion and carrot for 8 minutes with the cumin.
2. Add the lentils and stock and simmer for 20 minutes.
3. Blend until smooth and finish with lemon juice.

## Notes
Freezes well.
