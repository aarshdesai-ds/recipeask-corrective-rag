## Ingredients
- prawns: 250 g
- linguine: 200 g
- garlic: 4 clove
- chilli: 1 pcs
- lemon: 1 pcs

## Method
1. Cook the linguine.
2. Fry the garlic and chilli, add the prawns for 3 minutes.
3. Toss with the pasta and lemon juice.

## Notes
Ready in the time the pasta takes.
