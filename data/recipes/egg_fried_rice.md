## Ingredients
- rice: 150 g
- eggs: 2 pcs
- peas: 150 g
- soy sauce: 2 tbsp
- spring onion: 3 pcs

## Method
1. Cook the rice and let it cool.
2. Scramble the eggs in a hot pan, add the rice and peas and fry for 5 minutes.
3. Add the soy sauce and spring onion.

## Notes
Day-old rice works best. Soy sauce contains wheat.
