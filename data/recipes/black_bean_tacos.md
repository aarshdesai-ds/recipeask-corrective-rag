## Ingredients
- black beans: 1 can
- flour tortillas: 6 pcs
- red onion: 1 pcs
- avocado: 1 pcs
- lime: 1 pcs
- cumin: 1 tsp

## Method
1. Warm the beans with the cumin and lightly mash them.
2. Slice the onion and avocado and squeeze over the lime.
3. Fill the warm tortillas.

## Notes
Corn tortillas would make it gluten-free, but this recipe uses flour tortillas.
