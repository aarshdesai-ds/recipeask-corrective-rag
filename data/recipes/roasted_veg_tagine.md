## Ingredients
- sweet potato: 2 pcs
- chickpeas: 1 can
- dried apricots: 80 g
- ras el hanout: 2 tsp
- chopped tomatoes: 1 can
- couscous: 150 g

## Method
1. Roast the cubed sweet potato for 25 minutes.
2. Simmer the tomatoes, chickpeas, apricots and spice for 20 minutes, then add the sweet potato.
3. Serve on the couscous.

## Notes
Couscous is made from wheat.
