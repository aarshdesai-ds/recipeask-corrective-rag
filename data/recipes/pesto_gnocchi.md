## Ingredients
- gnocchi: 500 g
- pesto: 100 g
- cherry tomatoes: 200 g
- parmesan: 30 g

## Method
1. Fry the gnocchi until golden.
2. Add the halved tomatoes for 2 minutes.
3. Stir through the pesto and top with parmesan.

## Notes
Pesto is made with pine nuts.
