## Ingredients
- spinach: 300 g
- paneer: 250 g
- cream: 100 ml
- onion: 1 pcs
- garlic: 3 clove
- garam masala: 1 tsp

## Method
1. Wilt the spinach and blend it.
2. Fry the onion, garlic and garam masala, then add the spinach and cream.
3. Add the cubed paneer and warm through for 5 minutes.

## Notes
Uses a lot of spinach.
