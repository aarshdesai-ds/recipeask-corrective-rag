## Ingredients
- chicken breast: 400 g
- panko: 100 g
- eggs: 1 pcs
- flour: 50 g
- katsu curry paste: 2 tbsp
- rice: 150 g

## Method
1. Coat the chicken in flour, egg and panko.
2. Bake for 25 minutes.
3. Make the sauce from the paste and serve with the rice.

## Notes
Panko is made from wheat bread.
