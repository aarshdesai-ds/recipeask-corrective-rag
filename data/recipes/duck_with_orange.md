## Ingredients
- duck breast: 2 pcs
- orange: 2 pcs
- honey: 1 tbsp
- potatoes: 500 g

## Method
1. Roast the potatoes for 45 minutes.
2. Score the duck and cook it skin-side down for 12 minutes, then turn for 4.
3. Reduce the orange juice and honey in the pan into a sauce.

## Notes
Rest the duck for 10 minutes before slicing.
