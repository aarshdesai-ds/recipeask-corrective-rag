## Ingredients
- halloumi: 225 g
- courgette: 1 pcs
- red pepper: 2 pcs
- red onion: 1 pcs
- cherry tomatoes: 200 g

## Method
1. Roast the chopped vegetables for 20 minutes.
2. Add the sliced halloumi and roast for 10 more minutes.

## Notes
One tray, little washing up.
