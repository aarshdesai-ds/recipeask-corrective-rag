## Ingredients
- pasta: 200 g
- spinach: 150 g
- basil: 20 g
- cashews: 40 g
- garlic: 1 clove
- lemon: 1 pcs

## Method
1. Cook the pasta.
2. Blend the spinach, basil, cashews, garlic and lemon juice with a splash of pasta water.
3. Toss the sauce through the pasta.

## Notes
The sauce is mostly spinach.
