## Ingredients
- beef mince: 500 g
- kidney beans: 1 can
- chopped tomatoes: 1 can
- onion: 1 pcs
- chilli powder: 2 tsp
- rice: 150 g

## Method
1. Brown the mince with the onion.
2. Add the chilli powder, tomatoes and beans and simmer for 35 minutes.
3. Serve with the rice.

## Notes
Better the next day.
