## Ingredients
- chicken thighs: 500 g
- lemon: 1 pcs
- garlic: 3 clove
- potatoes: 500 g
- rosemary: 1 tsp

## Method
1. Toss the chicken and halved potatoes with garlic, rosemary and lemon.
2. Roast for 25 minutes.

## Notes
Naturally gluten-free.
