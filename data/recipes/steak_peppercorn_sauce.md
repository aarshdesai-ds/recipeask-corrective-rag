## Ingredients
- sirloin steak: 2 pcs
- cream: 100 ml
- peppercorns: 1 tbsp
- potatoes: 500 g
- butter: 30 g

## Method
1. Roast the potatoes for 25 minutes.
2. Sear the steaks for 3 minutes a side and rest them.
3. Crush the peppercorns into the pan with the butter and cream and simmer for 2 minutes.

## Notes
A quick special-occasion dinner. Gluten-free.
